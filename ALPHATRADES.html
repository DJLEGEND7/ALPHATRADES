Our process

Plan. Execute. Grow.

Three stages, in this order, every time. It's the discipline that separates a strategy from a guess — and it shapes everything we teach inside the community.

01 / PLAN

Build the thesis

Every trade starts on paper — bias, structure, invalidation level, and position size decided before a chart is even open live.

02 / EXECUTE

Trade the plan

Entries are shared with the reasoning behind them, not just the ticket. Members see the setup, the risk, and the exit — as it happens.

03 / GROW

Review and compound

Every week closes with a review — what worked, what didn't, and what changes for next week. Edge is built in the review, not the entry.
